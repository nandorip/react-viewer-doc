# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work.
- Assume good intent, and clarify when something is ambiguous.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior does not meet this standard.
